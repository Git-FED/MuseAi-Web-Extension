# Privacy Policy

**Muse AI Sidebar** does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The extension has no server, analytics, advertising, user accounts, or remote code. It uses the browser's `sidePanel` permission to display its local interface and opens `https://muse.ai/` in a new tab when requested by the user. Any data entered on the Muse AI website is governed by Muse AI's own privacy policy and terms, not by this extension.

This policy is effective as of November 12, 2026. Questions about this extension can be directed to the repository maintainer.
